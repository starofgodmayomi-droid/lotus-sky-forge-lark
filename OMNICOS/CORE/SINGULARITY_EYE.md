# SINGULARITY EYE

Every intent walks the same four steps. The eye does not skip the comparison.

1. PERCEIVE — KAI shield. Lexicon: `urgent`, `send money`, `guaranteed`, `private key`. A hit halts. Status DIVERGENT. The intent is not executed.
2. CONNECT — ELION routes what remains. Local limbs may run: checksum, Ease(t), SHA-256, sealed text, ledger count. Remote limbs are named and not called.
3. SYNCHRONIZE — OMEGA hashes the observed bytes with SHA-256. Expected ≡ observed → VERIFIED. They differ → DIVERGENT. Nothing was observed → UNKNOWN.
4. EVOLVE — the receipt is sealed onto the KAI chain. The previous hash is inside the next hash. A broken link is reported, not repaired by invention.
